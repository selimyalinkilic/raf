To your phone, without the setup

Found something on the laptop, want it on the bus? Pick whatever's easiest.

  • Telegram, one tap

    Settings → Connect Telegram → press Start. Send pages to your chat; send links to the bot and they land on your shelf. /last /today /search answer from it.

  • Chrome bookmarks

    Saved pages also go to Bookmarks › Raf, and Chrome sync brings them to your phone. Nothing to install.

  • QR code

    Point the phone camera at the popup and the page opens.

  • Email

    A ready draft in your mail app, Gmail or Outlook. Just press Send.

Two ways to use Raf

Just the extension

  • Install and go, nothing to set up
  • Your shelf lives in your browser (export / import anytime)
  • Telegram, bookmarks, QR and email to reach your phone
  • Video from Instagram, TikTok and plain video files

With your own Raf server

  • The same shelf on every device, plus a web app for the phone
  • YouTube, X, Reddit and 1000+ sites, quality choice, audio only
  • Always-on Telegram bot with a daily digest
  • One docker compose up, your data stays on your machine

Install in a minute

  1. 1
    Download & unzip

    Get raf-extension.zip from the latest release and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.

  2. 2
    Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).

  3. 3
    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked, pick the folder, then pin Raf from the puzzle icon.

Download raf-extension.zip Run your own server

To update: unzip the new release over the same folder and press ↻ on the Raf card. Chrome Web Store version coming soon.

Your shelf is yours

No account, no analytics, no ads. Without a server everything stays in your browser. With a server, it stays on your server. The optional Telegram bot only passes messages along and deletes them as soon as your browser picks them up.
